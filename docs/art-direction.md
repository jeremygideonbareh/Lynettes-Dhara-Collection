# The thread

Lynette calls her Shillong boutique a “Boutique at Home”. The opening feels like unfolding a piece across a table. A fine line runs from the masthead into the range index and down to the ordering instructions.

The hero pairs a sculpted drape with a large Cormorant Garamond nameplate. DM Sans carries captions, prices and actions. Deep maroon, warm ivory and muted gold come from the brief. Product photography retains its original colours. Only source photographs and clearly identified artistic studies appear.

The page moves from the boutique and its five range names to individual pieces, a rotating lookbook, fabric details, then WhatsApp and visiting information. Visitors can go straight to the collection from the masthead. Each individual enquiry carries its source link to help the boutique identify the piece.

Lenis drives desktop scroll, with GSAP ScrollTrigger for reveals and pins. Three.js adds motion to the hero where supported. Touch gets native swipe and scroll. Reduced motion uses a still cover and direct transitions.
