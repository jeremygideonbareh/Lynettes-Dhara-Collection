## Archive coverage

The public feed and reels grid exposed **19 distinct posts**. Their post embeds yielded **61 original photos, slides and reel covers**. The public profile embed reported **383 posts**. Scrolling the signed-out grid ended at Instagram’s sign-up prompt. The public profile embed includes six posts and no pagination cursor. Further signed-out feed requests did not return catalogue data. Exact account follower counts differed across the public responses and have been omitted from the site.

The catalogue presents **53 photographed item or colour references from nine posts**. A reference identifies an exact source slide or reel and, for side-by-side entries, the labelled colour. Separate photographs can represent the same physical stock across different posts; the owner can reconcile confirmed SKUs before launch. Ten posts were excluded after review. The original JPEGs remain in local working storage. The site uses responsive WebPs with colour-label crops, full comparison images and detailed alt text.

- [Source inventory](source-inventory.csv) covers all 61 retrieved post images, their dimensions, post dates, public source links, catalogue IDs and inclusion or exclusion reasons.
- [Asset manifest](asset-manifest.csv) records the published hero and gallery files, responsive variants, item references, colour descriptions and caption evidence. Blank range, price and fabric fields mean the source does not establish those facts.
- [Range cover sources](range-sources.json) records the 150 × 150 official highlight covers used for range navigation. The five price-highlight covers were reviewed separately.
- Source dates for the six profile-embed posts use the published timestamps converted to Shillong time. Dates for other posts come from Instagram’s official post metadata, which does not state a timezone.

## Textile observations

The crimson roll on slide 10 has two distinct gold-toned bands; the teal roll on slide 8 carries narrow bands with green and magenta. Several lighter pieces show low-contrast stripes. These characteristics can be seen without making claims about fibre, weaving technique or cultural meaning. [New Arrivals carousel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DfzonKjmInI/)

The comparative photographs name both colours on each slide. The pair shots preserve the boutique’s own shade labels, including “Deep Teal Green”, “Peacock Teal”, “Plum Wine” and “Burgundy Maroon”. [Colour comparison](https://www.instagram.com/p/Df2H1rtD5Wd/)

The boutique identifies Ryndia with a printed pad in two separate carousels and labels four photographed pieces as Half Khor. The Premium reel identifies an ivory Dhara, maroon pad and size 38–39. Its caption mentions a discount without a price.
